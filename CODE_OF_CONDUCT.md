# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept feedback kindly, and focus on what is best for the people who use Tuzat. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer [@abdumalikyaqub](https://github.com/abdumalikyaqub) on GitHub. Reports are handled privately and fairly.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who repeat it.
